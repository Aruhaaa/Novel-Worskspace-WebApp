## Intent

Show Novelist Workspace as it is. This is a tour, not a sales pitch: no hype, no price, no "sign up now". The viewer should leave knowing what the website and the phone app actually do, because they watched the real screens do it. Calm, warm and unhurried, like the app itself ("A little room. A whole world.").

The user first asked how the promo would be, was shown five promo concepts, and then said "don't need promo then, just do the other one", meaning the show-it-as-is version. There must be sound.

## Assets

- Real screens of the website, captured from the running app in local mode with a demo novel: the editor, chapter index, notebook, story outline, tracker with writing sprints, manuscript import, export, the public library and the reader.
- Real screens of the phone app (Flutter): discover, novel page, reader, write, notebook, Me.
- The app's own words, taken from its screens: "A little room. A whole world.", "Two rooms, one door.", "Your manuscript, your notes and your world in one quiet place."

## Customizations

- **What to show:** the website (write, plan, track and sprint, import, read and share) and the phone app (read and write). Everything shown is real: nothing invented, nothing rebuilt in HTML.
- **Sound:** narration (text-to-speech), a soft music bed, and small sound effects (typing, page turn, a chime for the sprint). The music sits under the voice.
- **Proposed structure, for the plan review:** the tour is organised as chapters, using the app's own chapter index as the contents page: Write, Plan, Track, Read, Two doors (website and phone). The user may change this at the plan review.

## Notes

- Effort level chosen by the user: extra high, one agent. Do not fan out many agents; build frames one after another.
- Rendering stays user-gated: render only after the user approves the final preview.
- Do not show real personal data. The demo novel and its author are made up for the video.
- Any download (voice model, music) is announced to the user before it happens.
