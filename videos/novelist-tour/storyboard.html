Each cell is a static layout at its key moment, drawn in the video’s own colours and type; the small pictures are real screenshots. Nothing moves here: the note under each cell says what moves first.

Rules for this video

  • Real screens only. Nothing redrawn, nothing invented.
  • No sales words: no price, no “sign up now”, no hype.
  • No fast cuts, whooshes or bounce. Slow and quiet.
  • No stock photos, no glow, no gradients.
  • One held frame: the ending stays still while the last chord rings.
