About Novelist Workspace

A quiet place to write a novel, keep its world in order, and share it with readers.

Writing a novel is long work. The chapters live in one file, the characters in another, the timeline on a scrap of paper, and the feedback in a dozen messages. Novelist Workspace puts all of it in one calm space so the writer can think about the story instead of the tools.

For writers

  • Write. A distraction-free editor with chapters, autosave, version history you can go back to, and writing sprints with a timer.
  • Plan. An outline, a storyboard and a timeline, so you can see the shape of the book before and while you write it.
  • Imagine. A world notebook for characters, places and things, which you can mention inside a chapter and see on a relationship map.
  • Track. Word-count goals, daily streaks and a record of the sessions that got the book written.
  • Share. Send a private reading link to a beta reader, who can read and comment without making an account. Or publish a novel to the library.
  • Take it with you. Export a clean manuscript or a print-ready copy at any time. Your work is yours.

For readers

The Public Library is free to read and needs no account. Readers can follow a novel chapter by chapter, leave comments and reviews, like what they enjoy, and find new writers. Anyone who prefers to only read can choose the Read space and never see the writing tools.

Where it works

In the browser, as a Windows desktop app, and on Android. A guest can start without an account and keep work on their own device. An account keeps novels in the cloud so they follow you between devices. The interface is in English and Bahasa Melayu.

Who makes it

Novelist Workspace is an independent project by Kakamond. It is still growing, and it is built by listening to the writers who use it. If something is missing, broken or confusing, please say so on the contact page.
