Contact

Questions, problems, ideas, or something that should not be on the site.

Email

hello@kakamond.com

Novelist Workspace is run by one small team, so replies are not instant. Most messages get an answer within a few days.

What to write about

Something is broken

Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. The device and browser help too. Please do not send your password, and do not paste your whole manuscript. A short description is enough.

Content that should not be on the site

Every published novel, comment, review and profile in the app has a Report button, which is the fastest way to reach us. If a report is not possible, email us the address of the page and say what is wrong. We look at reports and remove what breaks the rules, for example harassment, someone else's work published without permission, or anything illegal.

Your data

You can export your work and delete your account yourself, from Preferences in the app. For anything else, such as a copy of what we hold about you, write to us from the address on your account so we can check it is you. The privacy policy explains what is kept and why.

Ideas, praise and feedback

Always welcome. The best ideas so far have come from writers describing what slowed them down.

If you are a writer who wants help with the app itself, the short guided tours inside the app are a good first stop.
