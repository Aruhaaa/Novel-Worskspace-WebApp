Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

What we keep, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it.

Novelist Workspace (“we”, “the site”) is run by Kakamond and available at novelistworkspace.kakamond.com, as a Windows app and as an Android app. This policy covers all of them. You can reach us at hello@kakamond.com.

1. What we collect

If you only look around, or use the app as a guest

Your novels, notes, settings and reading place are saved in your own browser or device storage and are not sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data deletes them, so export anything you want to keep.

If you make an account

  • Sign-in details: your email address and a password, or the name and email Google or Apple gives us if you choose that way of signing in. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are not readable by us.
  • Your writing: novels, chapters, version history, notes, outlines, world-notebook entries, goals and word-count records, saved so they follow you between devices.
  • Your profile: the pen name and the profile details you choose, and who you follow.
  • What you do with other people: comments, reviews, likes, messages, notifications, and the people you have blocked or reported.
  • Cover pictures: if you upload a picture as a novel's cover, it is stored with our database provider (Supabase). It is deleted when you replace or remove it, and when you delete your account.
  • Sharing: the private reading links you create, and the comments readers leave through them, with the name the reader typed in.

Technical information

  • Error reports. If something breaks, the app can send what the error said, which screen was open and which version of the app it was. It never includes what you wrote, your name, your email or the secret part of a link. You can turn it off in Preferences under “Help fix problems”. Reports are kept for 30 days.
  • Ordinary web logs. Our hosting provider and the other services below see your IP address and browser details when your device connects, as every website does.

2. What is public

You choose what to publish. A novel you publish and its cover picture, your pen name, your public profile, and the comments and reviews you post can be read by anyone, including people without an account. Do not put anything in them that you want to keep private. A private reading link can be opened by anyone who has the link, so share it only with people you trust, and you can switch it off at any time.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, save and show your work, and let people read and talk about novels.
  • To keep it safe: handle reports, blocking, and abuse.
  • To fix problems: understand errors and improve the app.
  • To send emails you need, such as password resets and account confirmations, and, if switched on, notification emails you chose.
  • To show advertising, where the site runs it (see section 5).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your manuscripts to train anything.

4. Who handles your data

We use these services to run the site. Each receives only what it needs for its job.

  • Supabase: database and sign-in for accounts.
  • Vercel: hosting of the website.
  • Resend: sending emails such as password resets.
  • Google and Apple: only if you choose “Continue with Google” or “Continue with Apple”.
  • Google Fonts and unpkg: typefaces and map styling files are loaded from these sites, so they see your IP address when a page loads.
  • Google AdSense: advertising (section 5).

These providers may process data in other countries. We do not allow them to use it for their own purposes beyond running their service for us, except as described for Google advertising below.

5. Advertising and cookies

The site includes Google AdSense. Where ads are shown, Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies and similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can see and change this in Google Ads Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also opt out of personalised ads from many companies at aboutads.info.

The app itself uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to remember that you are signed in, your settings and your work in progress. Blocking or clearing these will sign you out or remove local data.

6. Your choices

  • Export: download your manuscripts at any time from Manuscripts.
  • Delete: Preferences, then Account, then “Delete my account” permanently removes your account and everything in it: novels, chapters, notes, goals, version history, reading links, reviews, comments, messages and profile. It cannot be undone.
  • Notifications and emails: choose what you receive in Preferences.
  • Error reports: switch them off in Preferences.
  • Blocking and reporting: available on profiles, comments, reviews and conversations.
  • Other requests: to see, correct or remove other data we hold about you, email us from the address on your account.

7. How long we keep things

Your account data stays until you delete it. Error reports are removed after 30 days. Backups and logs at our providers can take a little longer to expire. We may keep a report about harmful content for as long as needed to deal with it.

8. Children

Novelist Workspace is not meant for children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets a higher age). If you think a child has made an account, tell us and we will remove it.

9. Security

We use well-known providers and take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it. Keep your password to yourself, and keep a copy of anything important by exporting it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes, and we will say so in the app where we can.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@kakamond.com, or the contact page.
