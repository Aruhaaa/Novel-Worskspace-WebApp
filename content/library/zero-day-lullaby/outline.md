# Outline: Zero-Day Lullaby

Present tense, close third. Day 1 is Monday; the summit is Day 9 (Tuesday). Chapters are Ilse's viewpoint unless marked Courier (Marit Soini) or Pille. The "eighth note" is the flat one that returns every 94 seconds; Jaan's music box has lost its eighth pin.

## Chapter 01: The Flat Note
Ilse. Harju Children's Hospital, neonatal ward, Mon 03:40. Purpose: hook the reader with a hum that is not a lullaby.
Beats: (1) Pille's call; she found Ilse's number on a card left Thursday by a courteous old man in a wrongly buttoned vest who brought kringel for the staff: "If the speakers ever sing, call Ilse." Ilse arrives through snow with a cardamom pod and a laptop bag. (2) Fourteen Pesa speakers hum one tune in unison. Ilse, deaf on the left, finds the source by feeling a cot rail tremble against her palm. (3) Pille jokes through fear: "Dead is very quiet, dear; this is worse." Knitted owls line every shelf. (4) Ilse walks the ward with a phone spectrogram and watches one note bend flat at the same moment every 94 seconds. (5) IT head Evald Olesk arrives in a puffer jacket and calls it a sound-pack glitch; Ilse lets him talk and notes which rooms he never mentions. (6) She unplugs one speaker; its neighbour carries the tune on without a stumble.
Closing beat: Ilse says it is not being played but read, the way a ticker is read, and the clock is still reading.
Plant: the flat eighth note; Pille's glance at the corridor camera.

## Chapter 02: Pebbles on a Shelf

Beats: (1) Third-floor spice warehouse, cinnamon in the floorboards; Helle's kringel warming on the radiator; Taavi in gloves ranting about firmware. (3) Jaan Tamm limps in, sees the word Pesa and goes the colour of porridge; he asks only which audio library. (4) Ilse and Taavi chart the recording and see the note bending in tiny, regular steps. (5) Jaan sends everyone to lunch and tells Ilse not to log it in the ticket system. (6) She opens a paper notebook, his old rule, while Helle's laugh carries up the stairs.
Closing beat: Jaan hands her a pencil: "Paper keeps its mouth shut."
Plant: Jaan's vest buttoned wrong; his key ring with a plain USB token (Tamm is Pesa's retained security contractor, so Jaan can sign firmware, once); the Nightjar folder on the high shelf; the music box on his desk with seven pins visible.

## Chapter 03: What the Tune Carries

Closing beat: she writes SIX COUNTRIES in the notebook and underlines it until the pencil snaps.
Plant: Rack 14-B; Taavi's flinch at the word "mole".
