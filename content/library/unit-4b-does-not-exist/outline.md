# Outline: Unit 4B Does Not Exist

Twenty-six chapters, all narrated by Dev Mahajan in first person, past tense, as a note to file written some months afterward. Each chapter needs about 2,230 words. Each carries at least one document as a block quote. Dates are for the year with Monday 6 October and Friday 21 November. See bible.md for the rules, costs and cast. Dev's corridor count log is in the bible; use it.

Structure at a glance: hook in chapter 1; inciting incident in chapter 3 (his own login has been paying 4B's rent); complications through chapter 12; midpoint reversal in chapter 13 (he made the door, and the room has been reading him); costs rising through 18; low point in chapter 18; the vigil and rescue in 19 and 20; climax in 24 and 25; the coda in 26.

## Chapter 01: Validation Error

- **Narrator, place, when:** Dev. Management Office, Harwick Terrace, then the fourth floor. Monday 6 October, 8:50 am to about 2 pm.
- **Opens on:** The first line of the note to file: what a note to file is, and the date. Then a red banner on a monitor.
- **Purpose:** Hook the reader with an impossible row, establish Dev's voice, the office comedy and the first physical wrongness.
- **Beats:**
  - Frame: Dev explains that a note to file is what you write when you want it on record that you told someone, dates it, and starts at the beginning. Go-live day for Tenantry 9. Noreen's banner (EMBRACE THE CHANGE), the butterscotch dish, Joyce ("Noted."), Espie filming a coffee.
  - 8:52 am: the validation sweep. Forty rows go green and one goes red: HT-4B. Document: the row. Tenant OCCUPANT, rent $1,840.00, balance 0.00, lease start 01/00/1900 ("the date a database invents when it has been told nothing"), FLOORPLAN_ID, PERMIT_NO and DOOR_COUNT blank. It has paid on time every month for as long as there have been records.
  - Lunch & Learn on Zoom with Brent from Tenantry: comedy of data hygiene. Brent mentions that any entry over $1,000 needs a Source of Funds ("it defaults to the preparer, so super easy"). Espie asks if it can auto-fix vibes. Brent, asked about the red row: "That's a migration ghost. Just delete it."
  - Dev quietly asks Joyce whether she knows 4B. "Noted." Then, as if she had been rude: "Four-B pays on the twentieth. In cash. I've never asked."
  - Dev takes a printed unit list up to the fourth floor in his lunch hour. Left sticks, the call button warm. The corridor: Chioma's mat at 4A, a bicycle in 4C, a smell of toast with nobody cooking. Where B should be, a red glass-fronted fire-hose cabinet. He paces 4A to 4C: nine. Feels foolish.
  - He walks back and gets twelve. He tries again: twelve. The carpet pattern between the doors is the same, but the diamonds seem to take longer.
- **Changes and closing beat:** He goes from a clerk with a data error to a man who has answered a wall. At the hose cabinet he hears two knocks from inside the plaster, unhurried, like someone confirming a delivery. He knocks back twice before he can think. Nothing. He goes downstairs and tells no one.
- **Plants and payoffs:** Plants Source of Funds (ch 25 picklist); Joyce's "Noted." and the twentieth (ch 3); the count 9 then 12 (ch 3, 4, 26); toast (ch 8, 13); Left sticking (ch 6, 12); the two knocks (ch 5, 7, 10, 14, 20); the inherited desk with a drawer holding a lemon-drop tin and a loose horn button (ch 3, 11, 13).

## Chapter 02: Delete This Row

- **Narrator, place, when:** Dev. The 14 bus, the office, the basement, Tolliver Court. Tuesday 7 October.
- **Opens on:** Wet umbrellas steaming on the 14 bus.
- **Purpose:** Establish the order to delete, Dev's refusal, Rafa's evasions, the building's chat, and end with proof that his login touched the row.
- **Beats:**
  - The 14 bus, 22 minutes, the rain. Dev's counting habit (22 steps to the stop) and the shape of his life: Tolliver Court 3F, two mugs, a stainless tiffin that was his mother's. He hasn't been to trivia in 21 months and doesn't say so.
  - Noreen's office (vision board, LIVE LAUGH LEASE). She tells him to delete the row: "It doesn't exist, hon. That's the whole point." She has a Post-it ready. Document: Pugh's email, subject in capitals: PHANTOM UNITS: ACTION REQUIRED BY 10/31. Dev sees that she is holding her lanyard with both hands. He remembers his interview, where she asked one question that was not on the form: who would we call? He had said his sister, "I guess," and she had written something next to it.
  - At his desk: the Delete dialog ("This action cannot be undone. Type the unit number to confirm."). He types 4, B, and stops. Joyce leaves a coffee. His reasons: he archives, never deletes; and when his mother first got sick the family was told for months that the sound they heard was nothing. He doesn't say that aloud.
  - Basement: Rafa's shop. The key board: 41 hooks, 40 keys, and on hook 4B a small brass key with a green tag that Rafa is oiling. "What's that one for?" "It's for the hook." "Depends who's asking." Comic standoff; Rafa asks if he's eaten.

  - 10:40 pm: Dev logs in from home and opens the audit trail on HT-4B.
- **Changes and closing beat:** He has refused a direct instruction. The audit trail reads: Last modified, 6 October, 23:58, by DMAHAJAN. He was asleep by eleven.
- **Plants and payoffs:** Deadline 31 October (ch 6, 16); the key on its hook (ch 11, 12); Mrs Han's quiet ones and the chat (ch 4, 16, 19); his mother's illness (ch 5, 20, 24); 23:58 (ch 3, 10, 12, 25).

## Chapter 03: Posted by Mahajan

- **Narrator, place, when:** Dev. The office, then 4A's doorstep. Wednesday 8 October.
- **Opens on:** A five-dollar bill held up to the light in Joyce's cubicle (series 1963).
- **Purpose:** Inciting incident. Dev discovers he has been paying 4B's rent for fourteen months without knowing it.
- **Beats:**
  - The audit trail in full: fourteen entries, the 19th of each month at 23:58, starting 19 August last year (his first day), RENT POSTED, CASH, $1,840.00, SOURCE OF FUNDS: PREPARER. Document: two lines of it. Under them is a fifteenth line that is not on the 19th: 6 October, 23:58, ACKNOWLEDGED, no amount, the night he knocked back twice. The Source of Funds column reads PREPARER on every line, even August last year, though RentWise had no such field (the migration filled it with the default, or with the truth).
  - Joyce with the deposit bag. A mixed envelope turns up in the night drop on the 20th each month: a 1957 silver certificate, a 1963 five, crisp twenties. A teller once asked if she had robbed a museum. "I reconcile what I'm shown." She mentions that Margie used to do the deposits. "Margie who?" "Dunleavy. Before you. I have her Christmas card somewhere." She cannot find it, and is rattled.
  - The paper receipts in the filing room, HT-4B-0825 onward, Received by D. Mahajan. The sevens are crossed in the way his mother taught him. He did not write them.
  - A dry exchange of emails with Pugh (document), Dev replying with the corporate stalling he is best at: "Reviewing the remediation path."
  - He goes to 4A on the pretext of verifying contact information. Chioma, off a night shift, in a hospital hoodie. Banter. She says the neighbors are quiet, quieter than 3B's television.   - He sits in his desk chair and opens the drawer. A lemon-drop tin with two left, a horn button the color of oatmeal. He understands he has been working for the room since the day he started.
- **Changes and closing beat:** He is no longer an observer. The line on the screen reads Posted by D. Mahajan, and it is the sort of sentence you cannot un-sign.
- **Plants and payoffs:** The 19th at 23:58 (ch 10); Source: PREPARER (ch 25); the crossed sevens (ch 11, 24); Joyce's missing memory of Margie (ch 11); the horn button (ch 13); the money (ch 10).

## Chapter 05: No Saved Messages

- **Narrator, place, when:** Dev. Tolliver Court 3F. Saturday 11 and Sunday 12 October.
- **Opens on:** A phone buzzing face down on a kitchen counter at 5:58 on a Sunday.
- **Purpose:** The quiet chapter. Make the reader love Dev and his sister, and take away the thing he never played.
- **Beats:**
  - Saturday: laundry, groceries, the 14 steps from his door to the stairwell, counting everything now. His apartment: a tidy, undecorated studio, his mother's steel tiffin, the unopened mail. A photograph he has not framed.
  - Sunday at six: the video call with Anjali. Tia has drawn Uncle Dev as a spreadsheet. Ben carves a pumpkin early. Anjali scans Mummy's recipe cards. She says "Okay. Okay." when she is frightened. She asks him to come for Diwali on the 20th and says Dad has asked. She notices that he has two mugs and one is clean. "Is something wrong?" "It's a data migration." She laughs, but not all the way.
  - After the call, his weekly ritual: thumb over one saved voicemail, MUMMY, 11 January last year, 9:41 pm, 0:23, never played. Tonight he decides to press it.
  - The voicemail list reads No Saved Messages. He searches cloud backup, carrier settings, an old phone in a drawer. Nothing. He had cleaned up some sixty rows that week for go-live, and works out only later that the voicemail was the cheapest thing he owned. Relief arrives first, then shame at the relief.
  - His apartment hallway: bathroom door was six steps from the bed. It is seven.
- **Changes and closing beat:** He has lost something because he did not hold it, and does not yet know the mechanism. Closing beat: sitting on the kitchen floor he says "Okay. Okay." in Anjali's voice to the empty room, and the radiator under the window knocks twice. He does not answer.

## Chapter 13: The Tenant of Record

- **Narrator, place, when:** Dev. 4B. Thursday 23 October, 02:20 onward.
- **Opens on:** A brass doorknob, warm as a hand.
- **Purpose:** Midpoint reversal. Dev did not find the room: he made it. Margie has been reading him. The question changes from what is it to what have I done and what will it cost.
- **Beats:**
  - Rafa gives him the key and will not go in. "I only oil it." Dev goes in alone and counts: nine paces from door to window.
  - The room: eleven feet wide, rain on the glass, oatmeal curtains in the bricked bay, a hot plate, two chairs, a radiator that knocks, a shelf of ten teacups, a clock stuck at 4:10. Toast and wet wool.
  - Margie Dunleavy, tall in a cardigan missing its third button. "You took your time." He gives her the button from the drawer. She gives him tea (the cup Grieve leaves on the ledge arrives warm). She knows his sevens. She has been reading his entries for fourteen months.
  - Reversal: "The room was always here, dear. It was a row that wanted a door, and you filled it in." Each clerk holds the door open while they hold the job; Ellis held his from '86, she hers from '99; when a clerk is filed the door shuts behind them. ## Mystery: solution, clues, red herrings

**Solution.** Unit 4B is a room the building keeps for people nobody is attending to. It is kept in existence by the rent roll, which the building obeys. Only the clerk's login has force; every entry is balanced out of the clerk's own least-held possessions, then the held ones. The clerk is the building's operating reserve. Eventually a clerk who writes long enough has nothing left to give and is filed as a tenant. The rent for 4B is the clerk's monthly neglect. The room can be made a real unit with a door only by completing the three required fields and paying a Source of Funds. Occupants can leave only if they choose to and if someone says their full name at the door.
